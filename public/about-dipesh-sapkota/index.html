Author and publisher

Dipesh Sapkota

Engineering student · AI/ML enthusiast · Builder

Dipesh Sapkota is the creator and publisher of Sportivo, an independent web application for discovering football, cricket, and basketball fixtures, checking Nepal Standard Time schedules, and reviewing available stream-source information.

Return to Sportivo to browse the homepage, football fixtures, cricket fixtures, and basketball fixtures.

Engineering and technology

Dipesh Sapkota is an engineering student at Thapathali Campus, Institute of Engineering, with public interests in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and practical software development. Publicly listed technologies include C, C++, Python, React, Next.js, and Vercel.

Sportivo reflects that practical approach: a focused React and Vite interface, responsive layouts, local Nepal time formatting, public fixture URLs, accessible navigation, and a deployment designed for fast web access.

Sportivo

Sportivo is built as a focused fixture desk with React and Vite and deployed for the web. The project brings sports schedules, match status, searchable team names, local kickoff times, and public fixture pages into one responsive experience.
